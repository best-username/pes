# Winning scripts and their best modifications

Examples teach the model better than long rule lists. Keep this file short: the current top winners
and, for each, the modifications that beat it. The data below is made up for the demo; replace it
with real winners from the ad account.

## W007: "The washer ate it again" (US)
- **hook:** My washing machine has eaten 43 socks this year.
- **first_line:** I counted. Forty-three singles in one drawer.
- **on_screen:** 43 lost socks | in one year
- **body:** Then I found socks with a tiny magnet on the cuffs. You snap the pair together, throw it in the wash, and it comes out as a pair. Every time.
- **cta:** Take the 30-second quiz to find your size.

Modifications that beat it:
- hook → "Where do the socks go? I finally found out." (curiosity instead of a number; CPC −14%)
- on_screen → "The drawer of singles | ends today" (names the scene the viewer recognises)

## W011: "Laundry day with three kids" (US)
- **hook:** Three kids. Fifteen socks. Zero pairs.
- **first_line:** That's every laundry day in our house.
- **on_screen:** 3 kids | 0 matching pairs
- **body:** These socks clip together with a small magnet. They go into the wash as a pair and come out as a pair, so sorting takes zero minutes.
- **cta:** Take the quiz and get five pairs for the whole family.

Modifications that beat it:
- first_line → "I used to spend twenty minutes matching them." (time cost, concrete)
